Questions

Before you ask.

How do I send my footage?

Any link works — Google Drive, Dropbox, or a direct upload link. Send the raw clips plus a short brief (platform, length, style or a reference video). We confirm receipt before your deposit link is issued.

What exactly does the 50% deposit cover?

Half up front reserves your editor and starts work. The other half is due only after you’ve reviewed the edit and approved delivery. Payment is via a Stripe link from TerminalSync.

What counts as a revision?

Changes to cuts, captions, music, pacing or on-screen text on the same video. Two rounds are included. A full re-edit from new footage counts as a new video.

Can I get a sample before committing?

Yes — request a sample edit from the top of this page. It’s the fastest way to judge pacing, captions and style on your own footage before you buy a package.

Do you also do dubbing or other languages?

That’s our sister service, Voice Studio by TerminalSync — video dubbing in Spanish and 15+ more languages. Ask us and we’ll bundle editing + dubbing for the same video.

What if I need more than 30 videos a month?

Email us for a custom volume quote. Agencies with steady volume get a dedicated queue and a per-video rate below the 30-pack price.
