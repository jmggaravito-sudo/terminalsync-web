## Draft copy (English; confirm whether a Spanish version is also required)

**Parties.** This agreement is between [legal provider name, address, and support contact], operating Voice Studio, and the customer identified in the account and final order. If accepting for an organization, you confirm you are authorized to do so.

**Service.** DUB provides Spanish dubbing using a catalog voice. LIP includes Spanish dubbing and an additional lip-sync pass, subject to footage review. Shorts or Reels are included only when itemized in your order. Voice cloning is not included and is not authorized under this agreement. The confirmed order sets the deliverables, price, taxes where applicable, schedule and revision terms.

**Content and permission.** For each order, you confirm that you have the rights or permissions necessary to submit and process the footage, audio, and appearances in it, and to use the resulting work for your intended purpose. You give Voice Studio permission to process that material and use its disclosed production providers only as needed to perform the service. No permission to publish your work as a sample, use it for marketing, or clone a voice is granted by this agreement.

**Quotes and payment.** A cart total is an estimate, and a request is not a purchase. We review the footage and send a final written quote for your approval before payment. Nothing is charged when you submit the request. Cancellation, refunds, and revisions follow [approved policy and mandatory rights]. No turnaround or result is promised beyond the confirmed order.

**Handling and delivery.** We provide the agreed outputs through the delivery method in your order. See [approved privacy notice] for the providers, processing, retention, and your choices. Contact [approved support contact] about delivery or data questions.

**Electronic acceptance.** By checking the acceptance box and submitting your request, you indicate agreement to this version. Keep a copy for your records. A new version requires a new acceptance; rights are confirmed separately for each request.
