## When to use

- You run a **Shopify** store and want to know which customers used to buy and stopped, without exporting a spreadsheet.
- You want a respectful outreach sequence written for you — not a single blast to everyone who ever bought.
- You want your best lapsed customers treated differently from one-time buyers, based on what they actually spent.

## What it does

Bundles two pieces that reinforce each other, in one install:

- **Shopify (the connector)** reads your real orders and customers — last purchase date, order count, amount spent — the raw material a win-back list needs.
- **Win Back Dormant Customers (the skill)** groups those customers by value and writes a short sequence — the reminder, the reason to return, the last call — without promising a recovery rate.

**A real example:** you want to close out the quarter and know your store has quiet regulars. You say *"which customers bought from my Shopify store before but haven't in the last 90 days?"*. Shopify pulls the order history and flags who's gone quiet and what they used to spend. Win Back Dormant Customers splits them into your best lapsed buyers and one-time shoppers, and writes a distinct short sequence for each group. You review the messages and send them through whatever channel you use.

## How to use

1. Install the Plugin and connect your Shopify store (store domain + admin access token).
2. Ask: *"list customers who haven't ordered in 90 days and what they used to spend"*.
3. Ask: *"write a win-back sequence for each group"* — review the messages before sending.

## Why the bundle works

The win-back skill alone needs a list of customers with last-purchase date and spend — data you'd otherwise have to export and clean by hand. The connector alone gives you that data, but doesn't know how to group it or what to say. Together: Shopify supplies the real numbers, the skill turns them into a segmented, respectful sequence — from "who went quiet" to a ready-to-send outreach, no spreadsheet in between.

## Limits

- It reads what's in Shopify's order history — customers who buy through other channels (in-store cash, a different platform) aren't visible to it.
- It doesn't promise a recovery rate or that dormant customers will return — it writes the outreach; your offer and audience decide the result.
- It doesn't message anyone on its own: you review and send through your own channel (email, WhatsApp, SMS).
- Requires connecting your Shopify store; it only sees what your admin access token allows.
