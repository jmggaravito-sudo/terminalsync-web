## When to use

- You want a batch of Facebook/Instagram ad ideas **and** the images for them, without hiring a designer or hand-writing prompts for an image generator.
- You run a small business, local shop, or online store and need ad creative ready to test this week.
- You want the AI to plan the ad copy and image direction, then actually produce the visual — not just a text description you have to build yourself.

## What it does

Bundles two pieces that reinforce each other, in one install:

- **Meta Ads Creator (the skill)** turns your offer into five distinct ad concepts — each with its own angle (problem-led, product-in-use, before/after, proof-led, mobile/home-video style), headline, body copy, the official Meta button, and a description of how the image should look.
- **Ideogram (the connector)** takes that image direction and generates the actual visual — clean typography, product mockups, poster-style compositions, or a remix into square/vertical formats — using your Ideogram account.

**A real example:** you say *"give me 5 Meta ads for my skincare line, with the copy and images, for a launch promo."* Meta Ads Creator writes the five concepts — including the headline text and what each image should show — and Ideogram generates a visual for each one, in the format you need (square feed, vertical feed, or Stories). You review the batch, keep the strongest ideas, and you have creative ready to upload.

## How to use

1. Install the Plugin and connect your Ideogram account (sign-in only, no API key).
2. Describe your offer: what you sell, to whom, in what area, at what price, and what you want the viewer to do.
3. Ask: *"give me 5 Meta ad concepts with copy and images for [your offer]"*.
4. Review the copy and images — replace any invented claim or number with a real one — and use the test plan from Meta Ads Creator to decide which to run first.

## Why the bundle works

Meta Ads Creator alone gives you a strong plan and a text description of the image — but then you still have to open an image tool, write your own prompt, and iterate until it matches. Ideogram alone can generate a great image, but it doesn't know what your ad should say or which five angles are worth testing. Together, the skill plans the batch and Ideogram renders it — from "what should I advertise" to "here are five ready-to-test ads" in one flow.

## Limits

- It doesn't guarantee sales, ROAS, or CPA — Meta Ads Creator flags assumptions and recommends testing, not promising results; the real numbers come from your ad account.
- Image generation runs on Ideogram's service and draws from your Ideogram plan/credits.
- It doesn't publish the ad for you — you still review the creative and run it through your own Meta Ads Manager.
- Requires connecting your Ideogram account; it only generates what that account and plan allow.
