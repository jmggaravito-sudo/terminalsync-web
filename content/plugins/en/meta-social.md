## When to use

- You post your own organic content to **Instagram and Facebook** and want a week's worth of captions written for you, then actually published — not just a document you still have to copy into each app.
- You run a small business, local shop, or service business and want variety (not five versions of the same offer) without hiring a social media manager.
- You want **final say before anything goes live** — publishing is public and can't be undone, so nothing posts without your OK.

## What it does

Bundles two pieces that reinforce each other, in one install:

- **Social Content Batch (the skill)** turns what you sell or do into a batch of posts — a mix of "what we do", behind-the-scenes, testimonial, tip, and offer/CTA — with platform-fit copy, format notes, hashtags, and a posting calendar for the platforms you actually said you use.
- **Meta Social (the connector)** takes the Instagram- and Facebook-ready posts from that batch and publishes them — image plus caption — to your Instagram and your Facebook Page, showing you exactly what will go out before it does.

**A real example:** you say *"give me 5 organic posts for Instagram and Facebook for the next two weeks, and post the first one now."* Social Content Batch writes the five posts — captions, format notes, hashtags, a day-by-day calendar — and Meta Social takes the first one, shows you the exact caption and image against your account, and waits for your OK. You approve, it posts. The other four stay ready for you to launch on their scheduled day.

## How to use

1. Install the Plugin and connect your Meta account (sign in with Facebook once — no API keys). Your Instagram needs to be a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page.
2. Describe what you sell or do, your tone, and which platforms you post to.
3. Ask: *"give me a week of organic posts for Instagram and Facebook, with captions and a calendar."*
4. When you're ready to publish one, ask the AI to post it — review the exact caption and image it will send, and approve before it goes live.

## Why the bundle works

Social Content Batch alone gives you a strong, varied batch of copy — but you still have to open each app, attach the image, and post it yourself. Meta Social alone can publish, but doesn't know what to say or when. Together: the skill plans a real week of content across the platforms you use, and the connector puts the Instagram/Facebook posts live with one approval each — from "what should I post this week" to published, without the copy-paste step.

## Limits

- **It never publishes without your approval** — every post is previewed exactly as it will appear, and posting is irreversible, so the AI waits for an explicit yes.
- The connector only publishes to **Instagram and Facebook**; the skill also writes LinkedIn and TikTok/Reels-script versions when you ask, but those you still post yourself — Meta Social doesn't reach those platforms.
- Your Instagram must be a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page, and you must be an admin of that Page — a personal Instagram can't publish through this connector.
- It doesn't promise likes, followers, or reach — those depend on the platform and your existing audience, not the caption.
- Requires connecting your Meta account; it only sees and posts to the Page and Instagram account that connection allows.
