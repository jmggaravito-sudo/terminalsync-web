## When to use

- You run a small business or shop, post your own organic content, and want a week or two of Instagram/Facebook posts written for you — not one caption at a time.
- Once you like a post, you want it to actually go out to your Instagram and Facebook Page without opening either app or re-typing the caption.
- You want a human approval step before anything public happens — this is not an auto-poster.

## What it does

Bundles two pieces that reinforce each other, in one install:

- **Social Content Batch (the skill)** turns what you sell or do into a batch of organic posts — a mix of content types, platform-fit copy for Instagram and Facebook, format notes for the image, hashtags scoped to your business, and a day-by-day posting calendar.
- **Meta Social (the connector)** takes the caption and image you approve and publishes it — to your Instagram, your Facebook Page, or both — through Meta's official Graph API. Nothing goes out until you say yes.

**A real example:** you say *"give me 5 organic posts for Instagram and Facebook for the next two weeks."* Social Content Batch writes the batch — captions, format notes, hashtags, cadence — and closes with a verdict on which post is strongest to publish first. You pick that one, attach your photo, and say *"post this to both."* Meta Social shows you exactly what will go out and to which account, and only publishes after you confirm.

## How to use

1. Install the Plugin and connect Meta Social — sign in once with Facebook, no API key to paste.
2. Describe what you sell or do, your brand voice, and that you post to Instagram/Facebook.
3. Ask: *"Give me 5 organic posts for Instagram and Facebook for the next two weeks, with captions, format notes, and a calendar."*
4. Pick a post from the batch, add your own photo, and ask to publish it — review the preview and confirm before it goes live.

## Why the bundle works

Social Content Batch alone gives you strong copy and a plan, but you'd still copy-paste it into the Instagram and Facebook apps by hand. Meta Social alone can publish, but has no idea what to say or when. Together: the skill plans a real batch instead of one caption, and the connector is what actually gets it in front of your audience — with you approving every post before it's public.

## Limits

- Meta Social only publishes organic posts — no scheduling, no comment replies, no ad spend, and it can't touch anything beyond what you approve.
- Your Instagram must be a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page you admin; a personal Instagram can't publish through the API.
- Social Content Batch doesn't promise reach, followers, or virality — those depend on the platform and your existing audience, not the caption.
- Publishing is irreversible once it's live, so every post requires your explicit confirmation before it sends.
