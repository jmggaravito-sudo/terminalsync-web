## When to use

- You post your own Instagram and Facebook, and the hard part is deciding what to post — not taking the photo.
- You want a batch: a week or two of varied posts, not one caption at a time, and you'd rather not open either app to publish them.
- You want to stay in charge — nothing goes out until you approve it.

## What it does

Bundles two pieces that reinforce each other, in one install:

- **Social Content Batch (the skill)** turns what you sell into a batch of organic posts: a real mix of post types (what we do, behind-the-scenes, customer proof, a tip, a direct offer), platform-fit captions, format notes for the image or video, hashtags scoped to your niche and location, and a cadence across one or two weeks.
- **Meta Social (the connector)** publishes the Instagram and Facebook ones — image plus caption, to your Facebook Page and your linked Instagram — showing you exactly what will go out and to which account. Nothing publishes until you say yes.

**A real example:** Sunday night you say *"give me 8 organic posts for Instagram and Facebook for the next two weeks — I run a small bakery, warm tone, and I have the photos."* Social Content Batch writes the captions, the format notes for each photo, the hashtags, and the calendar. Monday you pick the first post, attach the photo, approve the preview — and it's on both feeds.

## How to use

1. Connect Meta Social in the app: you sign in with Facebook and grant posting permission — no API keys to paste. Your Instagram has to be a Business or Creator account linked to your Facebook Page, and you need to be an admin of that Page.
2. Ask: *"give me a week of organic posts for Instagram and Facebook for [what you sell], with captions, format notes, hashtags, and a calendar."*
3. Swap in your own photos, then ask it to publish the day's post — and approve the preview.

## Why the bundle works

The skill writes a batch you'd still have to publish by hand, one post at a time, inside the apps. The connector publishes — but on its own it has nothing to say until you dictate a caption. Together, the two surfaces the connector actually reaches (Instagram and Facebook) are two of the platforms the batch was written for: the captions arrive platform-fit, and publishing them is an approval instead of a copy-paste session.

## Limits

- Publishing covers **Instagram and Facebook Page only**. The batch's LinkedIn or TikTok copy is yours to post where it belongs, and short-form video script outlines aren't publishable through this connector — it posts an image and a caption.
- No scheduling, no comment replies, no metrics, and nothing that spends money: organic publishing only, one post at a time, each behind your approval.
- Publishing is public and can't be undone — that's why the approval step is not optional.
- The skill doesn't promise reach, followers, or engagement. It writes the content; your audience decides what happens next.
