## When to use

- You want to know about new Google reviews and reply well, and you also want more of your happy customers to actually leave one — without doing both by hand in different tools.
- You run a local business and manage your own Google Business Profile.
- You want every public reply gated behind your OK, and you'd never fake, buy, or trade a discount for a specific rating.

## What it does

Bundles two pieces that reinforce each other, in one install:

- **Google Business (the connector)** reads your reviews — rating, comment, replied or not — and drafts a reply for each, posting only after you confirm.
- **Ask for Reviews (the skill)** spots the customers most likely to leave a good review, times the ask right after a good experience, and writes the message with the direct link — plus one polite follow-up, never a fake review or a paid-for rating.

**A real example:** three new reviews came in this week, one of them a 2-star, and you had a dozen customers with a good repeat purchase you haven't asked yet. You say *"what's new on my reviews, and draft a reply to the 2-star"*. The connector lists the three, flags the 2-star as unanswered, and drafts a warm reply that acknowledges the problem — waiting for your OK. Then you say *"these 12 customers had a great week, write the review ask for WhatsApp"* and Ask for Reviews drafts the message and a single follow-up for the ones who don't respond.

## How to use

1. Install the Plugin and connect Google Business (Settings → Integrations → Google Business).
2. Ask: *"any new reviews this week?"* and *"draft a reply to the latest one"* — review the draft, then confirm to post.
3. Share who recently had a good experience, and ask: *"write the review request for these customers, for WhatsApp"*.
4. Send the ask yourself and paste in the direct review link.

## Why the bundle works

The connector handles the reviews that already exist — reading them and replying once you approve — but it doesn't generate new ones. The skill proactively asks the right happy customers, but doesn't know what's already sitting unanswered in your inbox. Together they close the loop: react well to what's there, and go get more of what you want — instead of only reacting to whatever shows up.

## Limits

- **Replies only post after your confirmation** — nothing goes public automatically.
- The skill never fakes reviews, posts on a customer's behalf, or offers money or a discount tied to a specific rating — most platforms forbid it.
- Requires connecting your Google Business Profile (a Google Cloud project with Business Profile API access approved by Google) — the one genuinely fiddly setup step.
