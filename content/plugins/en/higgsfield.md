## When to use

- You want a Reels/Stories-style video ad — the UGC, home-video look that performs well on Meta — without filming anything yourself.
- You run a small business or online store and need video ad creative ready to test, alongside your usual static ads.
- You want the AI to plan the concept and copy, then actually generate the video clip — not just a written description of what the video should look like.

## What it does

Bundles two pieces that reinforce each other, in one install:

- **Meta Ads Creator (the skill)** builds five distinct ad concepts for your offer, one of them a mobile, home-video style angle built for Reels/Stories — with headline, body copy, the official Meta button, and a description of what the clip should show.
- **Higgsfield (the connector)** takes that direction and generates the actual video — up to 15 seconds, vertical 9:16, in a UGC style — or a supporting product shot, using your Higgsfield account and credits.

**A real example:** you say *"give me 5 Meta ads for my product, one of them a Reels-style video."* Meta Ads Creator writes the five concepts, including the home-video angle with its script beat and CTA, and Higgsfield generates the actual 9:16 video clip for it. You review the batch — static concepts plus a real video — and decide what to test first.

## How to use

1. Install the Plugin and connect your Higgsfield account (sign-in only, no API key).
2. Describe your offer: what you sell, to whom, in what area, at what price, and what you want the viewer to do.
3. Ask: *"give me 5 Meta ad concepts for [your offer], including a Reels-style video"*.
4. Review the copy and the generated video — replace any invented claim or number with a real one — and use the test plan from Meta Ads Creator to decide which to run first.

## Why the bundle works

Meta Ads Creator alone can describe the video you should make, but you'd still need to film or generate it yourself. Higgsfield alone can generate a strong video, but it doesn't know what your ad should say or which angle is worth testing first. Together, the skill plans the concept and script beat, and Higgsfield renders the actual clip — from "what should my video ad show" to a ready-to-review clip in one flow.

## Limits

- It doesn't guarantee sales, ROAS, or CPA — Meta Ads Creator flags assumptions and recommends testing, not promising results; the real numbers come from your ad account.
- Video generation runs on Higgsfield's service, draws from your Higgsfield plan/credits, and async video jobs can take longer than an image.
- It doesn't publish the ad for you — you still review the creative and run it through your own Meta Ads Manager.
- Requires connecting your Higgsfield account; it only generates what that account and plan allow.
