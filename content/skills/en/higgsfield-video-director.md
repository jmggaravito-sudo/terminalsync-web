## When to use

- You want a short UGC-style or product video for Instagram Reels/TikTok/Stories and you have the Higgsfield connector enabled (or plan to enable it) to generate it.
- You want a real shot plan — what's on screen, in what order, with what caption — not just a one-line "make me a video" prompt.
- You know Higgsfield generates video asynchronously (jobs can take longer than an image) and want the brief to account for that instead of assuming an instant result.
- You want to reuse a character or previous generation (Higgsfield's Soul/history features) for a consistent look across a campaign.

Do not use it to promise view counts, conversions, or ad performance — those depend on the account, audience, and spend, not on the brief. It does not claim a generation will look exactly as scripted; video models vary run to run.

## What it does

- **A shot-by-shot brief**: opening hook (first 1–2 seconds), what's shown in the middle, and the closing CTA frame — sized to fit inside Higgsfield's 15-second video cap, not written as if unlimited length were available.
- **Format and orientation**: vertical 9:16 for Reels/Stories/TikTok vs. a square or landscape cut when the placement calls for it, decided from where the video will run.
- **On-screen text**: the exact caption/CTA line to overlay, kept short enough to read in a few seconds, plus where to leave clear space for it.
- **Consistency plan**: when to reuse a trained character (Soul) or a prior generation as reference so a campaign's videos look like the same "person"/product across cuts, instead of restarting the look each time.
- **An async-aware test plan**: generate the strongest concept first, expect the render to take longer than an image, and what to check in the result (hook clarity, caption legibility, CTA visibility) before generating variations.
- **The verdict (always closes with this)**: a **0–100 score** for how ready this brief is to hand to Higgsfield, based only on what was provided; a threshold traffic light — 🟢 80+ generate now; 🟡 50–79 generate the strongest cut first while filling named gaps (product shot, brand tone, reference character); 🔴 <50 missing the basics (what's being sold, to whom, what action you want), not ready yet; **the single next action**; and the honesty caveat that the score reflects brief readiness, not a guarantee of how the render turns out or how it performs — Higgsfield's own credits/plan and your account's real numbers decide that.

This skill writes and structures the brief; when the Higgsfield connector is available it can hand the brief off to generate, but it does not control render quality or guarantee the output matches the script exactly.

## How to use

1. Describe the product/offer, the platform it's for (Reels, TikTok, Stories), and the action you want viewers to take.
2. Share what you have: a reference product photo, a character you've used before, brand tone, or past videos that worked. Say if you don't have any — the skill will flag the gap.
3. Ask for the brief: *"Give me a Higgsfield video brief for [product/offer], vertical, for [platform], with a clear CTA."*
4. Review the verdict before generating — fix flagged gaps or proceed knowing what's missing.
5. Generate the strongest cut first; use the reference/Soul reuse plan for follow-up videos instead of starting from zero each time.

## Best for

Small business owners and solo marketers who want scroll-stopping UGC-style video without a production crew, and agencies who want a structured starting brief before opening Higgsfield. Works best with the Higgsfield connector enabled and at least a rough product photo or description; without the connector, the brief is still usable to brief a human editor, but the skill won't generate anything itself.
