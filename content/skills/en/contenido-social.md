## When to use

- You run a business, shop, or service and need organic social content (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok) without hiring a social media manager.
- You don't want one caption at a time — you want a batch mapped across a week or two, with real variety instead of five versions of the same sales pitch.
- You can share what you sell or do, your brand voice, and which platforms you actually post to.

Do not use it to promise reach, followers, or virality — those depend on the platform's algorithm and your audience, not the caption. It writes content, it doesn't guarantee an audience.

If you gave what the business does, a rough tone, and at least one platform, that's enough to write the batch — it proceeds instead of stalling on clarifying questions for cosmetic details like exact hashtags or posting time.

## What it does

Builds a content batch, not a single post:

- **Mixes content types**: a "what we do" post, a behind-the-scenes, a customer proof/testimonial, a tip or value post, and a direct offer/CTA — not five identical pitches.
- **Writes platform-fit copy for the platforms you actually said you use** — a caption for Instagram/Facebook, a more professional LinkedIn rewrite, or a short-form video (Reels/TikTok) script outline. It doesn't pad every post with all three formats if you only asked for one or two platforms.
- **Gives format notes**: what the image or video should show, the aspect ratio, and where the hook goes (the first line, before "see more").
- **Suggests hashtags scoped to your business, niche, and location** — not generic high-volume tags with no relevance to what you do.
- **Lays out a cadence**: which post goes on which day over 1–2 weeks, spaced so the feed isn't all offers.
- **Flags the limits**: it does not promise likes, followers, or reach — those depend on the platform and your existing audience, not the copy.
- **Closes with a verdict (always)**: a 0–100 score for how ready the batch is to post given what you shared; a traffic light (🟢 80+ ready to schedule; 🟡 50–79 post the strongest 2–3 first and fill the gaps for the rest; 🔴 <50 too little about your business or voice to write anything that sounds like you); the single post to publish first; and the honesty caveat that the score is about batch readiness, not a promise of engagement or reach.

## How to use

1. Describe what you sell or do, your brand voice (casual, professional, funny), and which platforms you post to.
2. Share any proof you have: testimonials, before/afters, recent wins, or a promo you're already running.
3. Ask: *"Give me 5 organic posts for Instagram and Facebook for the next two weeks, with captions, format notes, and a posting calendar."*
4. Review the batch, swap in your own photos or video, and schedule from your own tool.

## Best for

Small business owners, local shops, service businesses, and solo marketers who post their own social content and want a week's worth of variety and a plan, instead of scrambling for what to post today.
